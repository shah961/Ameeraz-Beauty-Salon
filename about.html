About Ameeraz Beauty Salon

Dedicated to polished, personalized beauty experiences designed around every client's individual needs.

Our Positioning

Where Refinement Meets Individual Attention

Ameeraz Beauty Salon is dedicated to creating polished, personalized beauty experiences where every appointment is designed around the client's individual needs. Located on MM Alam Road in Gulberg, Lahore, our studio was conceived as an inviting retreat from the city's fast pace.

We believe true elegance stems from tailored care rather than standardized routines. From rejuvenating Hydra Facials and intensive Keratin treatments to refined makeup artistry for brides and special events, our focus remains steadfastly on client satisfaction, hygienic practices, and effortless style.

Our Principles

The Cornerstones of Our Care

1. Personal Consultation

We take time to listen to your preferences, analyze your hair and skin texture, and recommend treatments that suit your lifestyle.

2. Professional Hygiene

Cleanliness and sanitary precision are non-negotiable. Every instrument and workstation is prepared with utmost care.

3. Contemporary Artistry

Our technicians continuously refine their methods to offer contemporary styles while respecting classic aesthetic principles.

4. Client Comfort

From comfortable seating to a welcoming ambience, we strive to ensure every visit feels relaxing and rejuvenating.

The Space

Designed for Your Relaxation

Our salon layout combines privacy with an open, airy visual aesthetic. Neutral tones, soft lighting, and ergonomic seating ensure that whether you are spending twenty minutes or several hours with us, your comfort is prioritized.

Conveniently situated in Gulberg, Lahore, Ameeraz Beauty Salon provides an accessible location for residents and visitors seeking dependable beauty services.
